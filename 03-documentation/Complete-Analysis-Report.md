# Complete Analysis Report

## Executive Summary
Analyzed 6,037,904 bike rides over 12 months to understand 
member vs casual rider behavior.

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Members | Casual |
|---|---|---|
| Total Rides | 3,886,872 | 2,151,032 |
| Percentage | 64.3% | 35.7% |
| Avg Duration | 12.39 min | 21.24 min |
| Peak Day | Wednesday | Saturday |
| Peak Month | July | July |

## Recommendations
1. Weekend Premium Membership (10-20% conversion)
2. Classic Bike Loyalty Program (10-20% conversion)
3. Seasonal Campaigns (100K+ Year 1)

## Financial Impact
- Revenue increase: 40-80% (estimated)
- Member percentage: 75-85% (from 64.3%)
